# Granddaughters Learning Widgets Contract

### Track & Verdict
* **Classification:** `Track D — Hybrid Blend` (Interactive offline client widgets + modular bilingual learning apps).
* **Advisor Verdict:** Split into standalone focused widgets for expediency and cognitive simplicity. Younger granddaughter (5yo) focuses solely on 1–10 tactile vector tracing and counting. Addition challenge and alphabet modules are isolated and placed on hold.

### Active App Roadmap
* **App 1: Numbers & Vector Tracing (Active - index.html / widget_numbers.html):**
  * Target: 5-Year-Old ("Boo-Boo 1").
  * 100% focused on 1–10 tactile counting, Thai/Arabic twin cards, native human audio pronunciation, and authentic centerline vector tracing.
* **App 2: Math Addition Challenge (On Hold - widget_addition.html):**
  * Target: 7-Year-Old ("Boo-Boo 2").
  * Standalone visual addition puzzle (Grade 1-2 prep). Self-contained, tested, and archived on hold.
* **App 3: Alphabet & Spelling Studio (On Hold):**
  * Target: Future literacy module (ก-ฮ & A-Z). Planned in separate standalone widget.

### Hardware & Runtime Constraints

* No frameworks, no node_modules, no backend dependencies.
* Works seamlessly on budget Android and older iOS Safari browsers.
