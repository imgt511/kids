3. Phonetic Fuzzy Matching: Match childish slurs and elisions across all phonetic variants.

5. Stacking Syllables: Tone marks (like ้ Mai Tho) sit vertically above consonants (ด้), not in wide separate boxes.
6. Mom's 2-Second Gate: Settings cog requires a 2-second hold to prevent accidental child taps.
